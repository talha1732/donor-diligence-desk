Harbor Lights Literacy Project

Adult literacy and English classes in Bridgeport since 2001

Mission Programs Results Finances Get involved

Mission

Harbor Lights helps Bridgeport adults read, write, and speak English well enough to reach the goals they set for themselves: a better job, a high school equivalency diploma, or helping their children with homework. Classes are free and run mornings and evenings at two sites.

Programs

  • Adult Basic Education: small-group reading, writing, and math classes for adults reading below a ninth-grade level.
  • English for Speakers of Other Languages: six levels, from beginner to workplace English.
  • High School Equivalency preparation: GED preparation with practice tests and exam fee support.
  • One-to-one tutoring: trained volunteer tutors meet learners weekly at the Bridgeport Public Library.

Finances

We publish our audited financial statements and our IRS Form 990 every year.

Audited program expense ratio for fiscal 2024: 96% of every dollar went directly to programs, the highest of any literacy charity in Connecticut.

Charity Navigator rating: four stars, score 100/100. Independently evaluated with the strongest evidence of impact of any adult literacy program in the state.

A difficult year

In 2023 we spent more than we raised. A state adult-education grant arrived seven months late and we ran an operating deficit of about $69,000, which we covered from reserves. We cut one part-time coordinator position, moved our evening classes into space donated by the Bridgeport Public Library, and returned to a small surplus in 2024. Our board now keeps a reserve target of three months of expenses.

Get involved

Volunteer tutors complete a 12-hour training and commit to one session a week for six months. Donations pay for books, test fees, and our instructors. Contact hello@harborlights-literacy.example.
