About us

Riverbend Community Pantry provides free groceries, fresh produce, and benefits referrals to neighbors in New Haven's Fair Haven, Hill, and Newhallville neighborhoods. We run a client-choice pantry: families shop our shelves for what they need instead of receiving a pre-packed box.

We were founded in 2009 by members of three neighborhood congregations. Today we are an independent 501(c)(3) with a staff of nine and a volunteer corps that keeps our doors open six days a week.

Programs

Client-choice pantry

Our main site on Grand Avenue stocks staples, dairy, eggs, and fresh produce. Households can visit once a week.

Mobile Market

A refrigerated van brings produce and staples to two senior housing complexes and one school every week.

Weekend Backpacks

With four partner elementary schools, we send children home on Fridays with easy-to-prepare weekend meals.

SNAP and WIC enrollment help

Trained volunteers help families apply for SNAP and WIC benefits and connect them with utility assistance.

Our impact

What we counted at our distribution sites in 2024:

3,412 households served
486,000 pounds of food distributed
41% of food distributed was fresh produce
620 children in Weekend Backpacks
1,150 volunteers
14,300 volunteer hours

These are outputs, not outcomes. We have not yet measured whether household food security changes over time. In 2025 we plan to pilot the USDA six-item food security survey with clients who agree to take part.

Volunteer

  • Pantry shifts: stock shelves and help shoppers, Tuesday to Saturday
  • Mobile Market drivers: a valid Connecticut license and a clean driving record
  • Benefits navigators: we train you in a two-session course

Groups from schools, congregations, and workplaces are welcome. Email volunteer@riverbend-pantry.example to sign up.

Donate

Gifts of any size buy fresh produce, keep the van on the road, and pay our small staff. Every gift stays in Greater New Haven.

Give online or mail a check to Riverbend Community Pantry, 410 Grand Avenue, New Haven, CT.
